# Privacy Policy — Speak & Translate Pro

**Developer:** Anjum Learning Lab
**Extension:** Speak & Translate Pro — AI Voice to Text & Live Translator (Chrome extension, v1.1.3)
**Last updated:** 26 November 2026

## What this extension does

Speak & Translate Pro turns your speech or typed text into a translation using Google Gemini, and types the translation into the text box of the website you are using. It also offers dictation in your original language (hold Control and speak) and text-to-speech. It never presses Send on any website.

## Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. The extension has no account, no analytics, and no tracking. No translation history is saved.

## Data stored on your device

Your Gemini API key, language choices, selected model, and settings are stored only in your own browser (`chrome.storage.local`). They never leave your browser except as described below. You can remove the API key from Settings at any time, and uninstalling the extension removes this stored data.

## Data sent to third parties

- **Google Gemini API** (`generativelanguage.googleapis.com`): the text you translate, the audio you speak (Gemini AI speech engine), and your API key in a request header, only when you use translation, transcription, or Gemini text-to-speech.
- **Chrome's built-in Google speech service** (Browser speech engine): audio you speak, only when that engine is selected.
- **Google Translate** (`translate.googleapis.com`): the text being read aloud, only when you use the optional Free Cloud voice.

Nothing is sent anywhere else. Your API key is never put into web pages or logs.

## Permissions

The extension asks for access only to the supported websites (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, Copilot, DeepSeek, Meta AI, Muse, Poe, Mistral, WhatsApp Web, Gmail) so it can type translated or dictated text into their text boxes. Access to any other website is optional and only granted if you choose it in Chrome's extension settings.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same page and the extension will be updated accordingly.
